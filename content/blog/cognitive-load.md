Explore the profound influence of cognitive stress on match point execution. We examine how the nervous system reacts to high stakes and how this physiological shift dictates biomechanics.

### The Breakdown
When a player faces a breakpoint, their heart rate often spikes by 15-20 BPM. This sympathetic nervous system response triggers tunnel vision and muscle tension. The result? A perfectly grooved forehand suddenly lands in the net because the kinetic chain is broken by anxiety, not a lack of technical ability.

Training technical skills in a low-stress environment is fundamentally flawed if you cannot execute those skills when the biological alarm is sounding.
