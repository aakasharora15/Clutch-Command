### The Communication Gap
In doubles, pressure isn't just internal; it's shared. A breakdown in communication during critical points is often the silent killer of great teams. We've measured that in 30-30 situations, verbal communication between partners drops by 60%.

To build true doubles mastery, you must engineer routines that force communication even when the physiological urge is to retreat inwards.
