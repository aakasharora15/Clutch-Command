Delve into the transformation of tennis strategies, tracing the journey from traditional set plays to the era of analytical tactics. We explore how modern players are leveraging data to optimize their baseline positioning and shot selection under pressure.

### The Old Way vs. The New Way
Historically, baseline strategy relied heavily on intuition and repetitive drilling of set patterns. Coaches taught players to stick to their strengths regardless of the situation. Today, analytics reveal that cognitive load fundamentally alters shot execution on big points. 

Our findings indicate that during a 5-5 tiebreak, players instinctively step 2 feet further back behind the baseline compared to a 1-1 scoreline, sacrificing court positioning for perceived safety.

### Measuring the Clutch Quotient
By mapping heart rate data directly onto match footage, we can pinpoint the exact moment a player's strategy degrades. The elite players aren't necessarily hitting better shots—they are simply refusing to yield their court positioning when their biological alarm system tells them to retreat.
